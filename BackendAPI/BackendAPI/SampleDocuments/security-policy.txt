INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
============================

Document Reference: ISP-001
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 15 January 2026
Review Date: 15 January 2027

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy establishes the minimum security requirements for all information
systems owned or operated by the organisation. It exists to protect the
confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organisational data.

2. SCOPE
--------
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access
organisational systems or data.

3. AUTHENTICATION AND ACCESS CONTROL
-------------------------------------
  3.1 All users must authenticate with a unique username and password.
  3.2 Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase, lowercase,
      digits, and special characters.
  3.3 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all privileged accounts.
  3.4 Access rights must follow the principle of least privilege.
  3.5 User accounts must be reviewed quarterly and disabled promptly upon departure.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION
----------------------
  Level 1 — Public:       Information approved for unrestricted public release.
  Level 2 — Internal:     Information for internal use only.
  Level 3 — Confidential: Sensitive business or personal data requiring protection.
  Level 4 — Restricted:   Highly sensitive data requiring strict controls.

5. NETWORK SECURITY
-------------------
  5.1 All data in transit must be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  5.2 Remote access must use an approved VPN solution.
  5.3 Firewall rules must be reviewed every six months.

6. INCIDENT RESPONSE
--------------------
  6.1 All suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the
      IT Security team at security@example.com.
  6.2 The IT Security team will triage and respond within 4 business hours.
  6.3 A post-incident review must be completed within 10 business days.

7. COMPLIANCE
-------------
This policy aligns with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and applicable data protection
legislation. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.

8. REVIEW
---------
This policy is reviewed annually or following a significant security incident.

Approved by: Chief Information Security Officer
Date: 15 January 2026
